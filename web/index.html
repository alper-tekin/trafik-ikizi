Traffic Twin

One fixed camera over a real Los Angeles intersection becomes a calibrated traffic simulation. A Muse Spark agent then tests signal timings in that simulation and recommends one, with every claim tied to a measurement. Each step is scored against independent ground truth.

3 · Decide

A Muse Spark agent tests plans in the twin

The agent can only state numbers that came from its tools: the intersection summary, video queue evidence, the Webster textbook plan, and simulation runs on the video's own traffic (3 seeds of driver behaviour). A verifier then re-runs its recommendation, including on 10 random mornings the agent never saw.
